The QR step now has a "Copy Link" button under the QR code. It copies the link that the QR code shows, and shows a check icon for 1.5 seconds (or an error icon if the copy fails). Screen readers announce the result. While the WalletConnect modal loads, the "Open" button now shows a spinner instead of "Opening…", and keeps its width, name and keyboard focus.
